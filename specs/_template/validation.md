# Feature Validation

## Highest-risk behavior

Identify the security, authorization, data-integrity, workflow, or dependency failures that deserve the strongest evidence.

## Automated checks

List focused unit, integration, boundary, and build checks at the lowest reliable layer.

## Manual checks

Describe the user-visible journeys, accessibility review, and environment-specific checks that cannot be proven reliably in automation.

## Evidence

Record commands, environment category, results, and safe artifact references. Never record credentials or sensitive diagnostics.

## Merge gate

List the conditions that must be satisfied, or explicitly approved as exceptions, before merge.
